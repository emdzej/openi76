Interstate '76 in your browser

OpenI76 runs here as openi76.wasm on the gasm runtime: the same module as on the desktop, a port of the original game's code, reading the missions, cars, sounds and music from your own Interstate '76 discs. Nothing from the game is on this site, and nothing you pick leaves your computer.

A work in progress: the shell, the trip's and the training's missions run, with the AI, the weapons, the cockpit and the sound; most missions have not been played through yet and network play is not ported. See Status.

What you need

  • Interstate '76 for PC (Activision, 1997; the program I76.EXE version 1.0): disc 1 as an .iso image or its files copied off the disc, and disc 2 as an .iso, or as a .bin with its .cue for the music. See Game data.
  • A browser with WebAssembly and Web Workers: a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  • About 1.1 GB of browser storage to import both discs as images with the music (once; later visits start at once), or none to play without importing.
  • A keyboard and a mouse; a gamepad works too.

The browser may clear site data when disk space runs low.

Or choose the discs again below to replace them.

Pick the images together: disc 1's .iso, disc 2's .iso or .bin, and the .cue next to the .bin (how to make them). The discs are told apart by their labels. A folder is disc 1's files (I76.EXE, I76.ZFS, MISSIONS, ...), without disc 2.

    Importing copies what you picked into this site's private browser storage (OPFS). Playing without importing reads it from where it is, which you'll need to pick again next time.

    Controls

    The mouse drives the shell's menus. In a mission the game reads the keyboard with the original's default keys (input.map): the keypad's arrows drive (8 throttle, 2 brake, 4 6 steer), S starts the engine, . and , shift, Space (or the left mouse button) fires, Enter picks the next weapon, F1-F11 the cameras, Esc skips a movie or opens the menu. Change the keys in the Esc menu's Control Configuration. More in Playing.

    Holding Esc for a second stops the game. In fullscreen the browser takes Esc to leave fullscreen. The options and the keys are kept in this browser.
